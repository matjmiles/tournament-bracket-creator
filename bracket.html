Tournament Bracket Builder

Printable single- and double-elimination brackets, 2 to 32 teams.

Tournament
Format

In the print dialog choose Landscape, set scale to 100% / Actual size, and turn headers and footers off. Line up the ⊕ marks where pages overlap, then tape.

This online copy can preview brackets but can't print. To print, open bracket.html on your computer in Chrome or Edge.
